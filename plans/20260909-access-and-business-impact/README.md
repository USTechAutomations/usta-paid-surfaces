## What qualifies as a noticeable difference

A product should advance only when all of these are demonstrated:

1. **A qualified buyer gets a useful result.** Their actual, authorized input produces output accepted in their real workflow; synthetic fixtures establish engineering behavior only.
2. **The advantage matters.** It materially reduces a recurring cost, delay, error or missed opportunity compared with the buyer's existing process and free alternatives. The size of that improvement must be measured with the buyer, not guessed by a model.
3. **A repeatable route reaches suitable buyers.** Store search, relevant search queries, a useful embedded tool or an established inbound surface produces identifiable progression. Bots, our probes, impressions and generic downloads cannot substitute for this.
4. **The business collects and fulfills reliably.** Purchase, entitlement, useful output, recovery and canonical attribution agree; delivery does not require recurring operator labor.
5. **Something defensible accumulates.** Lawfully retained historical observations, better supported formats, reusable integrations, embedded distribution, trusted specialist usage or returning customers. More unrelated pages do not satisfy this.

The optional 90-day target was asked during this review. Until the operator chooses it, no dollar or customer target is treated as authorized doctrine. The ranking below favors repeat paid business use and material contribution. No simulation or traffic estimate is offered as a probability of success.

## Recommended order

**First, repair the shared route from payment to useful output and trustworthy measurement.** Keep existing income detection and fulfillment protected. Reuse the current Stripe key where appropriate; finish product-specific acceptance without spending money or inventing a new minter. Handle network failure as UNKNOWN and confirm recovery. The market manager cannot guide investment from absent or stale data.

**Then prove a small number of existing buyer workflows.** LedgerMatch and QRelay are good candidates because their stated tasks recur inside businesses; actual advantage and adoption remain unproven. A historical-data product can strengthen the data moat if a buyer needs changes unavailable in a convenient free source. Choose that product from an actual request, not from the number of rows we collected.

**Use the already accessible stores to test distribution.** KDP Lens, Grant Fit and the EPA actor have a credible place for interested users to find them. Their value and conversion still need evidence. Improve first useful use and paid delivery before adding AppSumo, AWS or more directories. Keep KDP's consumer audience separate from a claim of B2B client growth.

**Do not fund another broad build wave yet.** Every unproven product below needs a bounded decisive customer test. New work remains appropriate if a documented buyer problem exposes a material gap the existing estate cannot solve.
