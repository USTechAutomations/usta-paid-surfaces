What this page cannot tell you

  • This is 172,942 published rows we pulled on 26 Aug 2026, not the city's all-time file. A row we did not pull is not in the file you buy.
  • Person-name and contact columns are stripped from every file we sell. The portal still has them. We will not put them in a paid file.
  • A blank cell means the city left it blank. We do not fill it in.
  • Every address on this page is cut back to the street: we print 957 Fell St, never the flat or unit number the city prints after it. A suite or a PMB stays, because an office is not a home. Nothing has been removed from the city's file or from our copy of it -- this is a rule about what we republish, not about what we hold.
  • Layer 14 (Building Permits - 2022) and earlier layers on the same FeatureServer were not pulled. A year we did not pull is not in the file you buy.
