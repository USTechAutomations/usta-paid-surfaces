What “entered our record” means, and what it does not

It means the docket number is on a later copy of ours and was not on an earlier one. That is the whole claim. It is a fact about our copies, and you can check it against the two dates we print.

It does not mean the case was filed that day. Look at 3:26-cv-06951: the court says it was filed on 8 Jul 2026, and it first appears in one of our copies on 17 Jul 2026, nine days later. A search index takes its own time, and so do we.

It is not a finding about anyone named in a case. We hold what a public court records search returned on a given day. We do not score, rank or grade any company, and there are no letter grades anywhere in this feed.

Questions people ask before they pay

The court records site is free. Why pay you for it?

You are not paying for today’s search. You are paying for the answer it gave on a day that has since been overwritten.

Case 1:26-cv-02224 read “42:1981jb Job Discrimination (Race)” in our copy of 18 Jul 2026 and “42:2000e-2 Job Discrimination” the next day. Only the later one survives in the live search. If you ran the search yourself and kept the answers, you do not need us.

How do I know these are real cases?

Look up any docket number on this page in a public court records search and you will find the case. That is the half we cannot prove to you on a web page, and it is the half you are buying.

What we send is the copy itself with the date we sealed it, not our summary of it.

Is this every AI case in the federal courts?

No, and it is not close. We do not read courts. We run four saved searches on a court records site, and a case is here only if one of those searches returned it. A case nobody described in those words is not in this feed, and neither is any court those searches never returned.

Does a case appearing on your copy mean it was filed that day?

No. It means the case entered our record that day. The court’s own filing date is a separate column, and it is usually earlier — sometimes by weeks.

No. Our last copy is 13 Aug 2026 and collection has paused. Every run since has fetched nothing and saved nothing. What we hold today is the sealed archive of 34 dated copies, and we are not charging for it. Ask us where it stands and we will tell you straight.
