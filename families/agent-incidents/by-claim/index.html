What this page cannot tell you

  • A case turning up on a later copy means it entered our record that day. It does not mean it was filed that day. The court's own filing date is a separate column on these tables, and it is usually weeks earlier.
  • Our last copy is 24 Aug 2026. Collection has paused. The job has kept running every day since and has saved nothing at all, so no name and no count on this page moves until it starts again. We are not promising you tomorrow's file. 1 day inside our range has no copy at all either: 15 Jul 2026.
  • We do not read courts. We run 4 saved searches on a public court records site, out of 7 saved searches in all. A case only enters if one of those searches returns it, so a case about an AI system that nobody described in those words is not here, and neither is any court those searches never returned.
  • 2 of the 24 cases we hold carry no cause and no filing category in our copy. We leave those blank rather than filling them in from somewhere else.
  • We hold the docket list, not the case. There are no filings, no rulings, no dates of hearings and no outcomes in this feed.
  • A case being on this list is not a finding about anyone named in it. We hold what a public court records search returned on a given day, and we do not score, rank or grade any company on it.
  • These labels are administrative. A case filed under a patent code is not necessarily about the thing you would call an AI dispute, and we do not re-file it under a category of our own.
