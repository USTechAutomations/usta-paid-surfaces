Who published the data on this page

  • Every permit in this file was published by Austin, Texas, not by us. Source table: Issued Construction Permits.
  • Some of the permits here were published by the City of Austin, Texas. Austin puts this data in the public domain and asks — asks, not requires — that proper credit be given. So, plainly: this page uses material published by City of Austin, Texas - data.austintexas.gov.
  • This dataset's own metadata names the licence Public Domain U.S. Government. It does not note a restriction that would take the rows out of the public domain.
