Read this before anything else

This is a run sheet, not a till. It remembers who asked first, who has a seat, and who is waiting. When someone drops out, the next person on that list gets the seat. There is no “move someone up” step.

We never take a deposit, a no-show fee, or a card hold from an attendee. Not as a setting. Not later. If you need to sell tickets, this is the wrong product. Cash on the night, a transfer, a jar by the door, or nothing at all stays yours.

The sample below is an example from the prototype running on local fixtures, not a live gathering and not a live-web read. The names are made up. The seating order is what the engine printed.

Someone who runs the same gathering again and again, and the room has a hard limit: a weekly run, a monthly supper, a class of fifteen, a meal in a hall that holds forty. More people ask than fit. What this is not

  • Not a ticket shop.We do not sell your seats and we do not take a cut of the door.
  • Not a deposit, a no-show fee, or a card hold.Those are the usual answers to empty chairs. We will not build them. Repeat no-shows get a check-in, then have to reconfirm, then stop being put back on the list automatically.
  • Not a way to skip the queue.There is no override that moves a friend up. If you need someone in the room, add them before the list opens.
  • Not a live login today.After you pay, the buyer receives the sheet. There is no account to create on this page.
