What this page cannot tell you

  • We can only show a change between two of our own reads. USGS can revise an event whenever it likes, so if it changed a number and changed it back between two of our reads, we never saw it and it is not on this page.
  • Each file we saved holds the last 24 hours of events, and our runs were about 24 hours apart, so two copies sealed on consecutive days usually share no event at all: only 23 of our 71 consecutive day pairs had one in common. A before and an after exists only where two runs landed closer together than usual.
  • 13,844 of the 17,819 events we hold were caught on one day only. There is no second reading to compare them with, so they cannot show a revision.
  • We ran on 14 Aug 2026, 15 Aug 2026, 16 Aug 2026, 17 Aug 2026 and the feed gave us nothing back, so there is no copy for those days. We say that rather than skipping over it.
  • 4 days inside our range have no run at all: 4 Jul 2026, 15 Jul 2026, 22 Aug 2026, 23 Aug 2026.
  • We hold what USGS published. We are not saying which reading is right. The current catalogue keeps only the later one.
