What this page cannot tell you

We hold what we sealed, and nothing else. There are days inside our range with no copy at all, and there is nothing at all before our first copy or after our last one. If your event happened outside that window, or was revised outside it, we cannot help and we will say so in the first reply.

We do not know why a number changed. We can show you that USGS said one thing on one day and something else on another, both read out of copies we dated ourselves. We do not know what the analyst did in between, we never ring anybody up to ask, and we do not say which reading is the right one.

This is not a seismology service and it is not a warning system. It is a record of what one public catalog said on the days we copied it. Nothing on this page is a judgement about USGS, about any event, or about anybody’s building.

Questions people ask before they pay

USGS is free. So why did you charge for this?

We should not have. The argument was that USGS revises an event in place, so only today’s version is fetchable and our dated copies show what it said before. That is true as far as it goes, but USGS also serves superseded versions itself, so the gap we were charging to fill was mostly not a gap. We took it off sale on 25 Aug 2026.

The example still stands as a thing worth seeing: event hv75020387 read 2.33 on 18 Aug 2026 and 1.75 the next morning. That is what our seals record. Go to USGS first — it is their data and they publish the history. If our dated copies are still useful to you after that, ask and we will send what we hold.

How do I know these numbers are real?

Look up hv75020387 in the USGS catalog and you will find a real event with a magnitude of 1.75. The other half — what it said before — is what our seals hold, and it is the half we cannot show you on a web page.

What we send is the copy itself with the date we sealed it, not our summary of it. Where our copy is missing a day inside the window you asked for, the file names that day rather than skipping over it.

Do you hold the day I need?

Probably not for every date you want, and we say which up front.

We seal what we sealed. There are days inside our range with no copy, and the file you get names each one. Send us the event id and we will tell you which seals exist for it in a two-line reply.

What can you tell me about one event?

Name it by its event id and we tell you what USGS asserted at each seal we hold, and which days in its range we hold nothing for. A date window works too. There is no charge for any of it — email us.

You are a small company. What if you stop?

It would not change what we already sealed. This collector was switched off for a stretch, and on 23 Aug 2026 we turned it back on by a dated decision, so it is reading again now. Either way, what this page describes is dated copies we already hold.

It is a one-time file out of copies already taken, not a subscription to new ones. What we send is a plain file you keep that still opens if we are not here next year. Our oldest copy is 23 Jun 2026 and our newest is 24 Aug 2026, and there are 8 days inside that range we hold nothing for — 4 Jul, 15 Jul, 14 to 17 Aug, 22 Aug and 23 Aug. We name those up front.
