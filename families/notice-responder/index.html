  • CP2501Same mismatch, inquiry first. You must respond.IRS: CP2501
  • CP14You owe unpaid tax. Pay by the date on the notice.IRS: CP14
  • CP501First reminder of a balance still open.IRS: CP501
  • CP503Second reminder. A tax lien can follow.IRS: CP503
  • CP504Notice of intent to levy. Pay or call immediately.IRS: CP504
  • CP11Math or clerical correction. You owe.IRS: CP11
  • CP12Math or clerical correction. Refund changed.IRS: CP12
  • CP21AReturn changed after processing. You owe.IRS: CP21A
  • CP22AReturn changed after processing. Balance due.IRS: CP22A
  • CP161Unpaid balance. Call within 10 days if the figures are wrong.IRS: CP161
  • CP2100Payer notice: missing or mismatched payee TINs.IRS: CP2100 / CP2100A
  • LT11Final levy warning (Letter 1058). Collection Due Process hearing.IRS: LT11 / Letter 1058
  • LT16Collection of unpaid tax and/or missing returns.IRS: LT16
  • CP49Refund used to pay another tax debt.IRS: CP49
  • CP59No record of a prior-year personal return.IRS: CP59

Use the response date on your actual notice. This page does not calculate a deadline, prepare a letter or submit anything to an agency. An unknown code is not guessed; use the IRS general lookup at Understanding your IRS notice or letter.

Why letter packs are unavailable

A generated letter is not for sale. The lookup above restates the matching IRS page for 16 codes: what the notice means, the deadline language on that page, the response options listed there, and which papers to gather. It does not write a reply.

New purchases are disabled. There is no announced reopening date.
