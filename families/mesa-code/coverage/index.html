What this page cannot tell you

  • 1 ZIPs in this store have no page here. 84065. They are sealed the same way. A ZIP gets a page when it has at least 5 cases that changed status AND 5 that arrived, so a page is never one table plus a decoration. We will say what we hold for the rest in an email rather than guess on a page.
  • Mesa republishes about once a week. The second table is the whole of it: six days in a month on which the file was different. Every comparison in this feed is between two copies carrying different Mesa update stamps.
  • Out of the file is not closed, and into the file is not opened. Both counts on this page are about what is in Mesa's published file on the day we read it. Neither is a statement about what happened to the case.
  • No street address is printed on this page. Our sealed copies carry the address Mesa publishes for every case, and this page prints the case number and the ZIP instead. The case number is what Mesa’s own system looks a case up by, so a row here is one you can check against the city rather than one you have to take from us.
  • Mesa's own download is free and carries most of this. The same cases, with the opened and closed dates. What it cannot give you is what a case said on a past day, and that is the only thing this feed adds.
  • One city is not a market. This feed is Mesa, Arizona and nothing else. If you need another city we do not hold it, and we will say so rather than start collecting it and bill you for the wait.
