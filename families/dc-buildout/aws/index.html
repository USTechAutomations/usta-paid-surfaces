What this page cannot tell you

  • This is pictures, not permits. Nothing on this page is a filing, an approval, an announcement or a contract. It is what a satellite picture of the same square of ground showed on two different days. We publish no megawatt figure and no percent-built figure anywhere in this feed, because neither of those things is in a picture, and putting one next to a satellite reading would be inventing it.
  • Every picture covers the same fixed square, 65,536 pixels of it, centred on the site. That square takes in whatever else sits around the site — fields, roads, a car park, a neighbour's land. A move on this page is a move across the whole square. It is not a measurement inside a boundary, because we do not have the boundary.
  • Bare ground can fall because grass and crops grew back over the summer, and it can rise because a field beside the site was harvested or ploughed. We do not separate weather and farming from work on the site, and we will not pretend to. On a site surrounded by farmland, read a summer fall in bare ground as the season before you read it as anything else.
  • A cloudy picture is never compared. We only put two pictures side by side when at least half of each one is readable and no more than two fifths of it is cloud. 537 of the 671 pictures we hold clear that bar. The rest are kept and named on the page about pictures we could not use, not quietly dropped.
  • Five percentage points is our line and we chose it, so here is the arithmetic behind it. We can compare 416 pairs of pictures. 316 of them move by less than five points and 100 move by five or more. Below five points a reading is inside the week-to-week wobble of this measure, so we do not call it a change.
  • The day we sealed a picture is not the day the picture was taken. A satellite passes when it passes and the sky has to be clear, so a set sealed on one Monday can carry a picture taken up to 14 days earlier. Both dates are on every row above, and we never present the sealed date as the date the ground looked like that. In 5 of the 100 moves we publish, the later set's picture was in fact taken no later than the earlier set's, because a set carries the newest CLEAR picture rather than the newest one. Read the picture dates, not the sealed dates, when you check a move.
  • We read this list about once a week and the newest set we hold was sealed on 24 Aug 2026. We do not promise you next week's pictures on this page. If a week goes by without a sealed set, this page starts saying so in its own words at the top, and it will keep saying so until we read the list again.
  • The word AWS here is the first word of the site's own identifier in our store, printed under every site name above. It is how we file the site, not a statement about who owns it, who is building it or who will run it. We do not hold ownership and we do not publish a guess at it.
