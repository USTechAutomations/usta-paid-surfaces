Doing this yourself

Siting notices sit in county agendas, utility filings and press pages that get reorganised and taken down. Nobody publishes a list of what changed this month.

To build this yourself you would have to watch each source on a schedule and keep a dated copy of every page, because the version that proves the change is the one that gets replaced.

What this page cannot tell you

No megawatt figure for any campus is in what we sealed. The frozen campus list carries a name, a place and the month it was announced, and no size at all. The only megawatt column anywhere on this page is the grid connection queue, and that is a county total for everything waiting to plug in, not a reading of any one campus.

A permit list is not a list of data centres. What we read is the Texas environment agency’s own list of air-permit applications waiting on a decision. Plenty of what is on it has nothing to do with computing, and a campus that needs no air permit never appears at all.

An application dropping off is not a decision. The agency stops showing a row when it stops being pending and does not always say why. We can tell you it was on one dated copy and not on the next. We cannot tell you it was approved, refused or withdrawn, and we will not guess.
