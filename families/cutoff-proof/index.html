Read this before anything else

We measure one thing. We are not going to pretend it is three things. The number on the certificate is a score on a sealed exam. It is not a claim that the agent is accurate in general, that it does not make things up, that it is safe, or that it is better than anyone else’s.

The certificate cites the stated cutoff. We do not claim that is the true cutoff. Vendors state these dates loosely and revise them. If the true cutoff is later than the stated one, a question we treated as post-cutoff may already have been in the training data. There is no way to detect that from inside. We name the stated date and where we got it.

The seal proves the exam was not edited after it was sealed. It does not prove that a stranger witnessed the seal before grading. Until the hash is published, dated, somewhere public before grading, that part rests on our word, and we would rather say so here.
