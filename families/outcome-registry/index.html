Read this before anything else

UNKNOWN is an allowed verdict. A lot of real claims will land there, because two public sources often do not say the same word. That is the product working, not the product failing. We would rather print UNKNOWN than invent a yes or a no.

This is not a listing of a person. We do not look people up, we do not score a name, and we do not publish a dossier. You file a statement about a future outcome. The public record is that statement, its fingerprint, the named sources, and the verdict or UNKNOWN.

This is not a prediction-market board. We do not list contracts, we do not show prices, we do not hold anyone’s stake, and we do not pay anyone out on how a call lands. The fee is the same whichever way it lands. We are a scoreboard, not a bookmaker.

What a filing is

You send four things: the statement, the deadline, the rule that decides it, and at least two named public sources that will be read at that deadline. We refuse a filing that names the same source twice under different capital letters, because that would let one voice agree with itself.

  • We fingerprint it at once.The fingerprint is a SHA-256 hash of the exact filed text, taken at filing time. If anyone later changes the words, the fingerprint no longer matches and the filing is refused a verdict.
  • The deadline passes, then we read the named sources.A verdict appears only when every named source was read and every one said the same thing, ignoring only spacing and capital letters. A majority is not enough.
  • Disagreement, or a source we cannot read, is UNKNOWN.The record still publishes exactly what each source said, or that it said nothing. We do not pick a winner.
  • Anyone can read one public record for free.You pay to file. Searching every filing at once is the archive seat, quoted in the email thread, not on the rail.

Questions we are actually asked

What if my sources disagree? You get UNKNOWN, plus exactly what each source said. That is F-0002 above.

What if one source cannot be read? You get UNKNOWN. We will not fill in the missing voice. That is F-0003 above.

Can I edit the statement after filing? If the text changes, the fingerprint no longer matches and the filing is refused a verdict. Treat what you file as final.

Do you hold the money if two people have a stake on the side? No. We never hold it and we never pay it out. If you want a referee that does not touch the stakes, that is this.

Is the filing public? Yes. One public record is free to read. The searchable full archive is the monthly seat, quoted in the thread, not on the rail.

Is UNKNOWN a cop-out? It is the honest answer when the named sources do not all say the same thing. A crisp yes from us, when the sources did not agree, would be a guess wearing a verdict’s clothes.
