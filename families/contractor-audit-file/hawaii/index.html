Hawaii: the test in its own words HRS 383-6 · read 2026-09-08
What the prong asks aboutThe official wordsSource
Control over how the work is done
…exists unless and until it is shown to the satisfaction of the department of labor and industrial relations that: (1) The individual has been and will continue to be free from control or direction over the performance of such service, both under the individual's contract of hire and in fact;
HRS 383-6
Whether the work sits outside the hiring firm's usual business
(2) The service is either outside the usual course of the business for which the service is performed or that the service is performed outside of all the places of business of the enterprise for which the service is performed;
HRS 383-6
Whether the worker has an independent business of their own
and (3) The individual is customarily engaged in an independently established trade, occupation, profession, or business of the same nature as that involved in the contract of service.
