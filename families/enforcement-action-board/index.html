What this is

These are public enforcement boards. Each one lists the newest formal US EPA enforcement actions on record for one state, and one national board lists the 100 largest federal penalties in the last 90 days. Every row is reproduced from EPA exactly as published — the company or facility name, the most recent case date, the statute, the federal penalty, the outcome, and a link to EPA's own case report. There are no adjectives and no summary beyond the record.

Records are reproduced from EPA as published, may lag the source, and say nothing about guilt or current compliance. US Tech Automations is not EPA.

What we withhold, and what EPA does not give us

  • A facility named after a person is withheldWhere the name on a record reads as a person trading under their own name (a sole proprietor, “John Smith Farms”), we do not put it on a page. Each board says how many rows it held back and why.
  • No city, no streetEPA’s enforcement-case dataset carries no location for the party in the 41 fields it returns, so the boards show no town. The state a board is about is the state we searched EPA for.
  • The date is the latest milestone on recordA case carries up to four dates — filed, settled, lodged, closed — and often only one. We show the most recent present, and name which it is.

Where the records come from52 boards, sealed 2026-09-07

Every row is read from a dated copy of EPA’s Enforcement and Compliance History Online (ECHO) case search that we sealed ourselves, so a row cannot quietly change under the page after we published it. This build holds 51 state boards plus the national penalties board.

OSHA: The DOL catalog page answered 200, but pulling and size-guarding the OSHA inspection CSV is not built yet, so boards are EPA-only for now.
