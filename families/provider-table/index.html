What the table comparesregistered 24 Aug 2026

One row on the public California agent-for-service-of-process table: what this company's own pages say about the service, quoted with the date the page said it.

The market is companies that act as the agent for service of process for a California corporation. Each one gets a row, and every row has the same 3 columns as every other row. The columns are fixed when the market is registered, and they are:

  • Price a year, as they publish it
  • States they say they cover
  • Says it scans papers the same day

A column only one company has an entry in would be a better position wearing a different hat, so there is no such thing here. Depth a company might one day pay for lives in its own notes, never as a new column.

We checked whether we sell anything into this market before agreeing to referee it. That check was answered “clear” on 24 Aug 2026, and its own reason reads “no product of ours sells under the tag 'ca_agent_for_service_of_process', the market is not on the barred list, and the word check was answered in writing for: nothing”.

The order is the product

Alphabetical by name. Nothing else. Paying does not move anybody, and there is no up to move to - a company can only climb this list by renaming itself.

That sentence is not kept true by anybody remembering it. The thing that puts the table in order is only ever handed 3 pieces of information about a company, and whether it has paid us is not one of them. Here is the whole of what the sorter can see:

  • provider_id
  • name
  • added_on

What paying would buy, if anybody ever did, is depth, checking and speed of correction. There is no position to sell, so nobody can be sold one.

A square we could not stand up says exactly that. The words are “we could not confirm this”, and they are a statement about us, not about the company. The table has no way of saying a company got something wrong about itself, and it never will have one.

Why anybody needs one of these at allread 24 Aug 2026

The entry test for a market. This table is only run in markets where the law COMPELS a business to appoint somebody, because a compelled buyer does not have to be persuaded and a table they read at the moment of choosing is worth paying to be on. That claim is a claim about the law, so it is quoted from the published text, dated, and re-checked against the saved copy every time this file is loaded. If a quote stops reproducing, the market stops being one we may run a table in.

  • Every corporation has to name, on its filed statement, somebody who can be handed a lawsuit on its behalf.Cal. Corp. Code section 1502(b) - checked word for word against our saved copy of the statute, ca-corp-1502-fetched-2026-08-24.txt, on 24 Aug 2026.
  • A company incorporated somewhere else cannot legally do business inside California until it has filed for a certificate of qualification, and that filing has to name the agent.Cal. Corp. Code section 2105(a) - checked word for word against our saved copy of the statute, ca-corp-2105-fetched-2026-08-24.txt, on 24 Aug 2026.
  • The out-of-state company's filing has to carry the name of the agent, and the same limits on who that can be apply.Cal. Corp. Code section 2105(a)(6) - checked word for word against our saved copy of the statute, ca-corp-2105-fetched-2026-08-24.txt, on 24 Aug 2026.
  • A company can only be named as somebody's agent if it has already filed its own certificate saying where it can be found and who there may be handed the papers. That filing is what makes the market a market: it is a list of companies that have gone to the trouble.Cal. Corp. Code section 1505(a) - checked word for word against our saved copy of the statute, ca-corp-1505-fetched-2026-08-24.txt, on 24 Aug 2026.
  • Whether a corporation that has filed its own section 1505 certificate may then name ITSELF as its own agent is not something we have found written down either way. We have not read the case law and we do not know. The table says we do not know, in those words, rather than picking whichever answer suits a page that makes money from people buying an agent.Cal. Corp. Code sections 1502(b) and 1505 - not checked by us.

The number this whole thing is judged on, and it is noughtkill date 22 Nov 2026

How many named companies loaded their own row. The number the whole portfolio turns on.

Three tables are being built at once to answer one question: does being on a public comparison table make a company reach for a card, or did that happen once because of the subject rather than the mechanism? The measurement is this, and only this - a NAMED party loading its OWN row, read out of the server log.

So the honest thing has to be said first, in the code and on the report: a web log usually cannot tell you who somebody is. An address and a browser string are not a company. If this counted every plausible hit it would produce a big encouraging number made mostly of guesses, and a big encouraging number made of guesses is how a project stays alive for a year after it should have been killed.

FOUR ANSWERS PER HIT, and only the first one counts.

  • self_lookup PROVEN. One of three things is true: the visitor came from a link on that company's own website, or the address is inside a network block somebody wrote down as theirs, or the request carries a one-time token that was only ever given to them.
  • someone_else PROVEN NOT them: they came from a rival's site, or the browser string is a search engine's crawler.
  • cannot_tell Everything else, which will be most of it, and it is reported at the same size as the headline rather than tucked underneath it.
  • unreadable A line this could not parse. Counted and shown. A log reader that silently drops what it cannot read reports a quiet day when the format changed.

TWO COUNTS, NEVER MERGED: how many distinct COMPANIES looked themselves up, and how many HITS there were. One company refreshing its own row forty times is one company. The kill number is written in companies, so companies is the number that is compared to it.

A redirect and a not-found are not loads. A page that answered 301 was not read.

Nought companies have looked themselves up, and nought server lines have been read. No page of this table is live anywhere a company could find it, so there is nothing yet that a company could load and no log for anybody to read. That is a description of where this is, not a measurement of how it is doing.
