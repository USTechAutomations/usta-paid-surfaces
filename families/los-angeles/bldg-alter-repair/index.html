What this page cannot tell you

  • This is 1,000 published rows we pulled on 25 Aug 2026, not the city's all-time file. A row we did not pull is not in the file you buy.

  • Every address on this page is cut back to the street: we print 957 Fell St, never the flat or unit number the city prints after it. A suite or a PMB stays, because an office is not a home. Nothing has been removed from the city's file or from our copy of it -- this is a rule about what we republish, not about what we hold.
