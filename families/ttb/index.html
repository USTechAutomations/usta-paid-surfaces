Doing this yourself

The TTB publishes the permit list as it stands today and replaces it. The appearances it hands you free every week in its own short file. To get the disappearances and the quiet changes to permits already on the list, you would have to download the national file over and over, keep every old copy, and cut it down to your state yourself.

Miss one download and the permits that came and went between it and the next one never show up in any later file.

What you get in each file

  • Every permit that appeared, and every permit that stopped being listedPermit number, business name where we hold one, city, state, industry.
  • One state or territory you nameNot the national file you would have to cut down yourself.
  • Cancel any month by emailNo account to close, no notice period.

How it works

  1. You buy below, or you email us first and name the state or territory you follow.
  2. If you emailed, we send the same checkout link in that thread.
  3. Each week a person emails you the appear / disappear file, and names anything we could not collect.

We have read this file 4 times: 8 Aug 2026, 11 Aug 2026, 19 Aug 2026 and 25 Aug 2026. The gaps between those reads were 3 days, 8 days and 6 days. Not one of them was 7. Three gaps are not enough to know how often the file changes, so we do not claim a rhythm for it. The weekly figure on this page is what we promise to send you, not a rhythm we measured.
