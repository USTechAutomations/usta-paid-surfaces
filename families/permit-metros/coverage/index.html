What this page cannot tell you

  • 6 boards in this store have no page here yet. Baton Rouge, Louisiana; Cambridge, Massachusetts; Cincinnati, Ohio; Los Angeles, California; Marin County, California; Montgomery County, Maryland. We seal them the same way. We have not written pages for them, and we will say what we hold for one of them in an email rather than guess on a page.
  • No property owner is named on any of these pages. They name the permit, the address and, where the city gives one, the contractor.
  • 2 of the 6 cities give no contractor name. Chicago and San Francisco publish a house number and a street and no builder, so those pages can tell you where the job is and not who is on it.
  • Entered our copy is not issued today. Every city page prints both numbers: how many of the week's arrivals carry an issue date inside the window and how many were issued before it.
  • The street address and the contractor are today's values. They come from the live table the collector keeps, not from the dated copy, so a correction the city made after the seal is what you read.
  • This is what each city published, not what each city did. A permit a city never put on its public file is not here, and we cannot tell you how many of those there are.
  • Twelve places is not the country. If the metro you sell into is not on this list, we do not hold it, and we will say so in the reply rather than start collecting it and bill you for the wait.
