Who published the data on this page

  • Every permit on this page was published by the Austin permit board, not by us. We keep dated copies of what they put out; the permits, the addresses and the wording inside those rows are theirs.
  • Some of the permits here were published by the City of Austin, Texas. Austin puts this data in the public domain and asks — asks, not requires — that proper credit be given. So, plainly: this page uses material published by City of Austin, Texas - data.austintexas.gov.

What this page cannot tell you

  • In our copy is not issued today. A permit counted here is one that is in the 3 Sep 2026 copy and was not in the 27 Aug 2026 one. That is first seen by us. 30 of the 133 carry a city issue date older than the earlier copy.
  • The address and the contractor are today's values, not the sealed ones. The dated copy we seal carries the permit number, the status, the issue date, the type and the ZIP. The street address and the contractor come from the live table the collector keeps, joined on the same permit, so if the city corrected one of those after we sealed the copy you are reading the correction.
  • Austin does not name a contractor on every permit. 2,387 of the 2,576 permits we hold for Austin carry a contractor name; the rest say nothing about who is doing the work, and those cells read “not given” rather than being filled in.
  • No property owner is named. These pages name the permit, the address and, where the city gives one, the contractor. They do not name a person who owns a building. Every address on this page is cut back to the street: we print 1801 W ST Johns Ave, never the flat or unit number the city prints after it. A suite or a PMB stays, because an office is not a home. Nothing has been removed from the city's file or from our copy of it -- this is a rule about what we republish, not about what we hold.
  • The status words are Austin's own. We print them exactly as the copy sealed them and we do not translate them, so they cannot be lined up against another city's words.
  • 6 days between 7 Jun 2026 and 3 Sep 2026 have no sealed copy. Nothing was collected on those days, so a change that happened and reversed inside one of those gaps is not on this page.
  • This is the city's published board, not the city's whole record. We hold what Austin put on its public permit file on the day we read it. A permit the city never published is not here, and we cannot tell you how many of those there are.
