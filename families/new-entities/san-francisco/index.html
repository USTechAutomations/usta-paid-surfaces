What this page cannot tell you

  • We keep each filing once, on the day we first see it. That means this page can show you what appeared. It cannot show you a company that was taken off the city's list, and it cannot show a field changing on a company we already hold. This file has been running thin: only 4 companies arrived on 23 Aug 2026, and 37 on 22 Aug 2026. So the table above widens to the last 12 arrivals rather than show you a table of 4.
  • This is San Francisco's own file and nothing else. It is not every company trading in the metro, and we do not claim it is. Every row on this page came from https://data.sfgov.org/resource/g8m3-pdis.json.
  • San Francisco's file never fills in what it is licensed to do, licence standing and map position, so those columns are not on this page at all. The city never published them and we will not invent them. San Francisco's file only sometimes fills these in: what the city calls the trade on 2,063 of 4,317 rows, trade code on 2,072 of 4,317 rows and postcode on 4,314 of 4,317 rows. Where a cell in the table is marked blank, that is the city's gap, not ours. Columns left off the table above because every row we are showing had them blank: what the city calls the trade.
  • Some rows are a person trading under their own name rather than a company. The city's file does not mark which, so neither do we. Every address on this page is cut back to the street: we print 957 Fell St, never the flat or unit number the city prints after it. A suite or a PMB stays, because an office is not a home. Nothing has been removed from the city's file or from our copy of it -- this is a rule about what we republish, not about what we hold. 1 row withheld: residential address. That one is counted across the 13 newest arrivals the table above was filled from, not across the rows printed above -- the printed table is the shorter list, and you cannot subtract one number from the other. Where the name on a record is a person's own and the address beside it carries a flat or unit number, we do not publish that row at all.
  • 11 of the 59 days between 26 Jun 2026 and 23 Aug 2026 brought back no file with rows in it: 4 Jul 2026, 8 Jul 2026, 15 Jul 2026, 8 Aug 2026 and a 7-day stretch from 11 Aug 2026 to 17 Aug 2026. A business registered and struck off inside one of those gaps was never in front of us, and a quiet stretch on this page is us not looking as often as it is the city being quiet.
