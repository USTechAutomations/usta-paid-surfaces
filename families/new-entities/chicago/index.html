What this page cannot tell you

  • We keep each filing once, on the day we first see it. That means this page can show you what appeared. It cannot show you a company that was taken off the city's list, and it cannot show a field changing on a company we already hold.
  • This is Chicago's own file and nothing else. It is not every company trading in the metro, and we do not claim it is. Every row on this page came from https://data.cityofchicago.org/resource/r5kz-chrr.json.
  • Chicago's file never fills in what the city calls the trade and trade code, so those columns are not on this page at all. The city never published them and we will not invent them. Chicago's file only sometimes fills these in: postcode on 3,620 of 3,626 rows and map position on 3,155 of 3,626 rows. Where a cell in the table is marked blank, that is the city's gap, not ours.
  • Some rows are a person trading under their own name rather than a company. The city's file does not mark which, so neither do we. Every address on this page is cut back to the street: we print 957 Fell St, never the flat or unit number the city prints after it. A suite or a PMB stays, because an office is not a home. Nothing has been removed from the city's file or from our copy of it -- this is a rule about what we republish, not about what we hold. One company can also appear more than once, because Chicago issues a separate licence for each thing a business is allowed to do. A name you already know can turn up again with a new licence, and the file you buy flags that rather than hiding it.
  • 11 of the 77 days between 26 Jun 2026 and 10 Sep 2026 brought back no file with rows in it: 4 Jul 2026, 8 Jul 2026, 15 Jul 2026, 8 Aug 2026 and a 7-day stretch from 11 Aug 2026 to 17 Aug 2026. A business registered and struck off inside one of those gaps was never in front of us, and a quiet stretch on this page is us not looking as often as it is the city being quiet.
