What this page cannot tell you

  • Classification questions cite the tariff schedule (HTSUS), not the CFR, so we do not draft explanations for those; they are counted above.
  • A question CBP withdrew ("all examinees granted credit") or answered two ways has no single explanation, and we do not invent one.
  • Machine-drafted explanations can be wrong. This is a study aid, not legal or tax advice, and not CBP's own explanation.
  • We hold nothing from a sitting before 2024-05-01. Older papers exist on CBP's page; we have not sealed a copy of them.
  • New sittings appear here only after we add the CBP PDFs by hand, which we do after each April and October exam.
