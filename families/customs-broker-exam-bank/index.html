What this is

This is the explained question bank for the last 5 sittings of the US Customs and Border Protection Customs Broker License Exam. Every question carries CBP's own official answer. Where the official key ties an answer to a Title 19 CFR rule, we add a short explanation that quotes that rule — fetched from the eCFR, drafted by a local model, and checked by a second local model. So far we explain 65 of 400 questions.

How the explanations are made

  • The answer is CBP's, not oursEvery answer letter comes straight from CBP's published answer key.
  • The rule is quoted, not recalledWe fetch the exact Title 19 CFR text the key cites from the eCFR and hand it to the model; the model quotes it.
  • Two models, not oneA first local model drafts the explanation; a second local model checks it against the same rule text. A flagged draft is withheld and counted, never shown.
  • We withhold rather than guessClassification questions cite the tariff schedule, not the CFR, so they get no explanation here — and we say so.

Where the questions come from

The questions and answer keys are CBP publications, in the public domain as US government works (17 U.S.C. 105). We downloaded them from CBP's own page.
