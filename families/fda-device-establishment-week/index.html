What you get

  • One national CSV every WednesdayEstablishments that appeared, stopped being listed, or changed since the previous weekly FDA export. Every country in one file.
  • Fourteen columnsExport date, FEI number, registration number, owner number, business name, city, state, country, what the site does, status, expiry year, how many product codes it lists, what kind of change, and which fields changed. No street address, no phone, no person's name.
  • The two export dates in every rowSo a row can be checked against the FDA register on the day.
  • An honest empty weekA week with no changes is sent as 0 plus the two export dates. A week where the FDA file could not be read says so instead of repeating the last one.

The sample on this page spans 129 days, not one week. The FDA overwrites its export every week and keeps no history. The oldest dated copy we could obtain is 1 May 2026, so the first comparison covers 1 May 2026 to 7 September 2026. Weekly files start with the first pair of copies we seal ourselves, one week apart.

The FDA gives the register away. You can download the whole file free at open.fda.gov any week. What you are paying for is the comparison: the dated copy it overwrote, and the list of what moved between the two. Registration is not FDA approval, and a row that stopped being listed is not proof of closure or of anything the FDA decided.

Newest copy read 7 September 2026. Rows from copies with missing identifiers, conflicting duplicates, or spreadsheet-unsafe values are left out and counted, never guessed.
