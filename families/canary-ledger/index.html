Read this before anything else

This is a dated factual record, not legal advice. A hit means your exact marker appeared in a specific document at a specific time, and the marker existed before that capture. That is the whole of what it can say. It does not name who copied you, it does not say a model was trained on your work, and it does not tell you what to do next.

Paraphrase will not hit. The marker survives only if the copy keeps your words. A rewrite, a summary, or an answer engine that puts it in its own words will never emit the marker, so we will find nothing — even if they read every line.

A clean sweep is not a clean bill of health. Finding nothing means nothing was found in the places we looked. It is not a promise that nobody copied you. We will never present it as one.

There is no retroactive proof. A marker planted today can evidence a copy made tomorrow. It can never evidence a copy made yesterday. If the copying already happened, today is the earliest you can start.

What $175 actually buys

The founding pack is a collection of minted markers for named pieces of your content, sealed with the time they were issued. After you pay, the buyer receives the markers. When a hit is proven, they email the evidence pack.

Minting on your own machine is free. The public file for each marker is a provenance certificate: owner, item, issue time, and a fingerprint (a hash) of the marker. The certificate never prints the marker itself. Publishing the marker would let anyone paste it onto a third-party page and fake a hit.

If you later want a weekly watch of pages you name, that is quoted at $49 per month in the email thread, not on the rail at the top of this page. That watch is not automated yet, and we are not fetching other people’s sites yet. Taking money for a live crawl without saying so is not on offer.
