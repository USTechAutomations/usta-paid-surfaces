Collection has paused and it is not starting again. The last copy of any kind in this record is 21 Aug 2026; the last copy carrying item numbers is 13 Aug 2026. The SEC keeps every filing itself, free and permanently, so reading the same list again every night would add nothing. This is a sealed record of what we saw on the days we saw it, and nothing new is being added to it.

Why we stopped rather than kept going. The SEC runs EDGAR, a free public archive that keeps every filing it has ever accepted, permanently, with no login. Re-reading the same list every night would have produced a second copy of something nobody can lose. So we sealed what we had and turned the collector off.

What that leaves worth having. Not access — you have that already. What this record does not contain

No accounting firm names and no people. An 8-K item 4.01 filing names the firm a company dropped and the firm it hired. An item 5.02 filing names the person. Our copy carries neither. It carries the item number, the company, the dates and the accession number, and that is the whole of it. Anyone selling you these as auditor names or executive names is selling you something this store does not hold.

The last three copies carry no item numbers. They came from the SEC daily index rather than the search list: 584 more 8-K filings we can name but cannot sort by what they reported. Nothing after 13 Aug 2026 appears on an event page.

Each read took about a hundred filings, not all of them. EDGAR’s search shows the newest documents first, and we sealed what it was showing. A filing pushed off that list between two of our reads never entered the record, so every count here is a floor.

3 days inside the item-coded window sealed nothing at all: 4 Jul 2026, 15 Jul 2026, 10 Aug 2026. We print the gaps rather than let you find them in the file.
