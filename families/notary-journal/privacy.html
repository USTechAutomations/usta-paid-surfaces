Plain-language notice for the Notary journal side panel browser extension.

Notary journal side panel is a browser extension. It shows your state's journal rule, quoted and cited, from a rules file packaged inside the extension, and keeps a 25-entry journal on this device. The extension makes no network requests.

Journal entries you type (signer names, document types, dates) are stored only in your browser with chrome.storage.local. They never leave your machine unless you export the CSV yourself. Removing the extension deletes them.

The extension does not read the pages open in your tabs. We do not sell or share anything, because nothing reaches us.
