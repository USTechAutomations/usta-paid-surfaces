What this page cannot tell you

A move is two copies disagreeing, and nothing more. A row here means the same public queue file said one thing on one dated copy and something else on the next. It is not an approval, a rejection, a decision or a filing. If you need to know why a project moved, the operator is the place to ask, and we will say so rather than guess for you.

The file you pay for is CAISO only. We read no in-service date out of the CAISO file: the column is empty on 2,278 of the 2,278 rows in our newest copy, so a CAISO move here is a status change or an entry or exit, never a date. Two of the queues we read have stopped coming. Our last MISO file is 6 Aug 2026 and our last ERCOT file is 30 Jul 2026. Every page built on those two says “last copy” and promises you nothing further.

The names and the fuels are the operators’ own words. Each operator writes the same fuel differently — Solar, SOL, SUN and S all turn up for the same thing. We print what they printed and mark a label we are not sure of as not clear, rather than tidying it into a category we invented.

Questions people ask

This data is public and free. What is this page for, then?

The data is not the hard part. Last week’s copy of it is.

CAISO publishes today’s queue and overwrites the last one. Fetch CAISO now and you get today. There is nowhere to fetch last Tuesday. We have been keeping a dated copy since 11 Jun 2026 — weekly at first, then one every day since 9 Aug 2026, 27 dated copies in all across the queues we read, sealed through 25 Aug 2026 — so the question “what moved” still has an answer. What this page shows is built out of those copies. If you already keep your own dated copies, you do not need us.

Are you allowed to collect this?

Everything here comes from a public queue file we fetched with a collector that says who it is.

Where a source says no, we stop, and we stop on the day we find out. MISO only serves a browser, and the only way to fetch it was to pretend to be one, so we read it ten times, the last on 6 Aug 2026, and stopped. What we sealed before those days is real and we still hold it, and nothing newer comes in from either. Both are named on this page instead of quietly missing. We could get around either one and we are not going to.
