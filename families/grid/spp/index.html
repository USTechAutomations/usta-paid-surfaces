What this page cannot tell you

  • Three state pages are not part of the subscription, and the 86 moves we hold for them are not in the weekly file. Over 74 days of collection, Kansas, Nebraska and Oklahoma produced no project that moved under a name. Those pages stay where they are and stay free to read — we are just not selling them.
  • SPP publishes no plant name at all. Every SPP row here is a number the operator itself uses — the queue number, like GEN-2024-340. The name column is empty on 826 of the 1,028 rows in its newest copy, and on the other 202 it holds a second study number rather than a name. Both numbers travel in the file you buy. We do not invent a name for a row that has none.
  • The operators write the same fuel differently — Solar, SOL, SUN and S all appear for the same thing. The fuel column is our reading of their labels, not their own count, and a label we are not sure of is shown as not clear rather than guessed at.
  • A move here means two dated copies of the same public file disagree. It is not a decision, an approval, or a filing. If you need to know why a project moved, the operator is the place to ask, not us. Email operations@ustechautomations.com and we will send you both dated copies so you can check ours.
