  • CAISO runs the power grid in California.
  • We hold 25 dated copies of the CAISO file, the first from 11 Jun 2026 and the newest from 24 Aug 2026, and 56,950 dated project rows inside them. The newest copy lists 2,278 projects.
  • Where those projects sit, by count: California 2,076, Nevada 104, Arizona 72, Mexico 18, Idaho 2.
  • 1 project in the CAISO queue moved between our newest copy and the newest copy at least a week older than it (17 Aug 2026 → 24 Aug 2026). We do not answer this with our last two copies any more: the newest two we hold are CAISO 1 day apart, and finding nothing over one day is much weaker evidence than finding nothing over a week.
  • One day we asked and came back with nothing usable: on 22 Aug 2026 the server answered with an error (503). We sealed no copy for that day, so it is skipped rather than compared against, and the rows either side of it are compared to each other.
  • Worth knowing before you build this yourself: we hold 25 dated copies of the CAISO file, so there are 24 gaps between them. The file itself came back different across 23 of those gaps, but a project had actually moved in only 8. Saving the files is the easy half. Telling which projects moved is the work.

Who published the data on this page

  • Every row on this page was published by a grid operator, not by us. Written out in full, they are California ISO. We keep dated copies of their public queue files; the projects, the names and the wording inside those rows are theirs.
  • The California ISO allows this use on the written condition that they are credited by name. So, plainly: this page uses material published by the California ISO.

What this page cannot tell you

  • We read no in-service date out of the CAISO file: the column is empty on 2,278 of the 2,278 rows in our newest copy. So a CAISO move here is a status change or an entry or exit, never a date. If in-service dates are what you are buying this for, say so at operations@ustechautomations.com before you pay, because for CAISO we do not hold them.
  • The operators write the same fuel differently — Solar, SOL, SUN and S all appear for the same thing. The fuel column is our reading of their labels, not their own count, and a label we are not sure of is shown as not clear rather than guessed at.
  • A move here means two dated copies of the same public file disagree. It is not a decision, an approval, or a filing. If you need to know why a project moved, the operator is the place to ask, not us. Email operations@ustechautomations.com and we will send you both dated copies so you can check ours.
