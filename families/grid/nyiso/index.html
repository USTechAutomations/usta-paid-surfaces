Who published the data on this page

  • Every row on this page was published by a grid operator, not by us. Written out in full, they are New York ISO. We keep dated copies of their public queue files; the projects, the names and the wording inside those rows are theirs.

What this page cannot tell you

  • When NYISO moves a project onto its withdrawn sheet, the in-service date disappears from the file, because that sheet has no such column. The date did not slip. We count that as the one withdrawal it is rather than a status change plus a date change, so 5 rows you might expect to see twice appear once.
  • The operators write the same fuel differently — Solar, SOL, SUN and S all appear for the same thing. The fuel column is our reading of their labels, not their own count, and a label we are not sure of is shown as not clear rather than guessed at.
  • A move here means two dated copies of the same public file disagree. It is not a decision, an approval, or a filing. If you need to know why a project moved, the operator is the place to ask, not us. Email operations@ustechautomations.com and we will send you both dated copies so you can check ours.
