What we actually hold

119,295 sealed rows, taken on 54 separate days between 23 Jun 2026 and 24 Aug 2026. The markets behind those rows are Kalshi 59,379, Polymarket 5,900, Manifold 3,013. Those two totals do not match, and that is the whole point: a Manifold market comes back in read after read, so we hold it many times over and can see it change. A Kalshi or Polymarket market shows up in exactly one read. The run log records 63 finished collection runs over those 54 days, because some days were read more than once.

We read every day, and 9 days did not work. Between 23 Jun 2026 and 24 Aug 2026 there are 9 days with nothing sealed at all, and the longest run was 6 days, 12 Aug 2026 to 17 Aug 2026. A market that resolved and dropped off a venue’s list inside one of those gaps is not in here, and we would rather print the gap than let you find it later.

Only 799 of our 59,379 Kalshi rows carry a question you can read. The rest are combination bets, and for those Kalshi’s own title is a list of the legs rather than a sentence. There is no question for us to print, so we do not invent one.

Polymarket does not give us a price at resolution. That field is empty on every Polymarket row we hold. We leave it empty rather than filling it in from somewhere else.

Doing this yourself

You can read any venue’s resolved list today. What you cannot read is the one from three weeks ago. None of these venues keeps a dated public copy of what a market said before it was edited, and a question that gets rewritten takes the old wording with it.

To have what is on this page you would have to pull three venues every single day, store every copy, and then compare your own copies against each other to find the ones that moved. Miss a week and the markets that resolved and dropped off inside that week never appear in any later file.
