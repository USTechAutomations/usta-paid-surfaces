What this page cannot tell you

  • We can only show a market that changed if we happen to hold it on two different days. If a venue edited a question and changed it back between two of our reads, we would never see it.
  • We collect three venues and only three: Kalshi, Polymarket and Manifold. Nothing here covers any other prediction market.
  • We read every day, but not every day worked. Between 23 Jun 2026 and 24 Aug 2026 there are 9 days with nothing sealed at all, and the longest run was 6 days, 12 Aug 2026 to 17 Aug 2026.
  • Each read takes the newest resolved markets a venue is showing, not every market that venue has ever resolved. A market that resolved and dropped off the list before our next read is not in here.
  • Questions are typed by whoever opened the market. We print them as they were written, spelling mistakes and all, because changing a word would make the row worth less than nothing.
