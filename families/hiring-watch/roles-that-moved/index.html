What this page cannot tell you

  • We ask 236 companies for a jobs page and a careers page every day. That is the whole panel, and a company that is not on it cannot appear here.
  • On our newest copy we can read a real list of named roles from 3 of those 236 companies. This feed is those companies and no others. The rest publish their openings in a way we cannot read as a list, so we say nothing about them rather than guess.
  • 182 of the 320 page copies we stored on our newest read are cut short: they hit our 256 KiB size cap, so the end of the page is not in the copy. Cut is not the same as useless — 103 of the 176 copies we can read a list out of are themselves cut, because the block of data holding the list closed before the cut. What we will not do is read a list out of a block that did not close: if the cut lands inside the list we read nothing rather than a short list. The risk we cannot rule out is a company that splits its roles across two blocks and loses the second one to the cut.
  • 87 of the 309 company-and-page pairs we hold 60 or more copies of produce a brand new fingerprint on every single copy, because something trivial on the page changes each time. A fingerprint moving is not a hiring change and is never a row on these pages.
  • We can only show a change between two of our own copies. A role posted and taken down inside one day happened while we were not looking, and it is not here.
  • We read the company's own page. A role advertised only on a job board, on a social network, or by a recruiter is not on that page and so is not here.
  • The archive is missing 4 days between 10 Jun 2026 and 23 Aug 2026, when the collector did not run. A change across a missing day is dated to the two copies either side of it, so the gap is visible in the dates rather than hidden.
  • A retitle is not always a promotion. The smallest one we hold is “Security Operationis Engineer (Contractor)” becoming “Security Operations Engineer (Contractor)”, which is a wording fix and not a decision. We send the pair and let you judge.
