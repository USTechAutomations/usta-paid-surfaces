What it does

You send a link to a vendor. They fill in the questionnaire once. Their answers turn into a page anyone can check later, so the next customer who asks them the same questions gets pointed at the same page instead of a new form. The honest line is simple: we host your vendor's own answers. We never say a vendor is secure. We just hold what they told you and make it easy to find again.

What this is not

We host your vendor's own answers. We never say a vendor is secure. Nothing on this page is our judgment of anyone's security.

It is not an audit. No one from us reviews or checks the answers. It only holds what your vendor typed and shows it back.

There is no account or password. Each side gets a private link instead. Anyone who has the link can see that side's page.
