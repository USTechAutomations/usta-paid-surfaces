Sites that refuse our machine. Two of them do, and we say so rather than working around it:

  • EUR-Lex — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), official text202 (AWS WAF JavaScript challenge, 0 bytes of document). Nothing is quoted from this source. The Article 50 rows cite the European Commission's own Article 50 FAQ instead, and say so.
  • New York General Business Law § 1701 (AI Companion Models)403 to this host; read through the session's browser route. nysenate.gov answers 403 to an ordinary request from this host. That is recorded here as a fact. The words were read through the browser route, so refresh.py cannot re-check them from this machine and reports them as unchecked rather than as verified.
  • New York General Business Law § 1702 (AI Companion Models)403 to this host; read through the session's browser route. Same 403 wall as § 1701. Read through the browser route; not re-checkable from this machine.
  • Illinois Public Act 103-0804 (HB 3773), AI in employment decisionsTLS failure from this host (“unable to get local issuer certificate”); 403 when verification is skipped; the browser route also fails on the certificate. We could not read this document by any route we are allowed to use. The Illinois row therefore states that we hold no text for it and quotes nothing. It is listed as a CITE-CHECK in NOTES.md.
