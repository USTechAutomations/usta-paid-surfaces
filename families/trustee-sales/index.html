What this cannot tell you

Two questions people will ask that this feed genuinely cannot answer, said here rather than after you have paid attention to it.

Whether any sale actually happened. The list says when a sale is scheduled. It never says what happened at it. We hold no outcome, no winning bid and no new owner for any of these properties.

What it means when a file disappears. 601 of our 637 rows carried a sale date still in the future on the day we read them, so a file stopping is almost always its date arriving and it falling off the front. We have not built a page of vanished sales, because building one would mean inventing an event the data does not carry.

Doing this yourself

Anyone can open that list today, free, and read the next twenty sales. That part is not hard and we are not pretending it is.

The hard part is having June's copy. To prove a date moved you have to have been holding the old date before it changed, which means reading the list every day and keeping every copy, starting before the day you needed it.
