SchemaHand is a browser extension. You paste CREATE TABLE SQL into its side panel and it draws the relationship diagram and data dictionary on your machine. The SQL you paste stays in your browser. We do not send table names, columns, or any pasted text to our servers.

Export needs a key. When you enter a key, or a saved key needs rechecking, the extension sends only the key string to our check service (a small server we run on Google Cloud) to confirm it is one of ours. That request does not include your SQL, your name, or your email.

The extension does not read the pages open in your other tabs. It has no access to your database. A successful key check is cached in your browser with chrome.storage.local for up to 7 days so export keeps working offline. Nothing is sold or shared.
