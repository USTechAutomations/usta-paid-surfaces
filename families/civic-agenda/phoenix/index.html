What this page cannot tell you

  • On 27 of our 74 reads, Phoenix returned a meeting list with nothing on it. We ask for the meetings dated in the last 14 days and ahead, and on those days Phoenix had none. That is a short agenda, not a failed read: the read worked every time and the answer was an empty list, not an error. The newest read did have meetings in it.
  • We can only show you a change that happened between two of our reads. If a council put an agenda up and took it down again inside one day, we did not see it and we will not pretend we did. We missed 12 days: 19 Jun 2026, 4 Jul 2026, 8 Jul 2026, 15 Jul 2026 and 8 Aug 2026, plus a 7-day stretch from 11 Aug 2026 to 17 Aug 2026. Anything that moved and moved back inside one of those gaps is not in this feed, and we will not claim it is.
  • We hold six governments and no others: Chicago, Seattle, Austin, Phoenix, Columbus, and Los Angeles County.
  • We ask Phoenix for the meetings dated in the last 14 days and ahead, and for the items Phoenix edited in the last 7 days. That window is what we hold, not the whole docket. So a row leaving one of these lists means it left this file: it does not mean the meeting was cancelled, the item was killed, or that anyone named on it went away.
  • A meeting dropping off the list is usually the list rolling forward past the meeting date, not a withdrawal. That happened 13 times here and is kept out of the table above on purpose.
  • We record what the meeting list said. We do not download the agenda document itself, so we cannot tell you which line inside the PDF changed.
