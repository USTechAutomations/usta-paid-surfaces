What this page cannot tell you

  • We can only show you a change that happened between two of our reads. If a council put an agenda up and took it down again inside one day, we did not see it and we will not pretend we did. We missed 12 days: 19 Jun 2026, 4 Jul 2026, 8 Jul 2026, 15 Jul 2026 and 8 Aug 2026, plus a 7-day stretch from 11 Aug 2026 to 17 Aug 2026. Anything that moved and moved back inside one of those gaps is not in this feed, and we will not claim it is.
  • We hold eight governments and no others: Chicago, Seattle, King County, Mesa, Austin, Phoenix, Columbus, and Los Angeles County.
  • We ask Chicago for 3 pages of its meeting list and 5 pages of its item list, 100 rows a page. Our newest copy came back with 300 meetings and 500 items, both of them full, so the city's own lists are longer than what we hold. A row leaving one of these lists means it left this file: it does not mean the meeting was cancelled, the item was killed, or that anyone named on it went away.
  • A meeting dropping off the list is usually the list rolling forward past the meeting date, not a withdrawal. That happened 46 times here and is kept out of the table above on purpose.
  • We record what the meeting list said. We do not download the agenda document itself, so we cannot tell you which line inside the PDF changed.
