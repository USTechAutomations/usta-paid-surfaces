What this page cannot tell you

  • A name leaving this register means the register stopped listing it. It does not mean the company shut down, the server was switched off, or anything went wrong. We only know what the list said on the day.
  • We do not publish a score, a grade or a ranking of any named company, and we do not work one out. Several of the sources we read exist to sell exactly that, and 3 names whose product is a score are kept off the tables on these pages for the same reason. Those names are still inside every count above.
  • The register only tells us what a seller filed. We do not call the address to see whether anything answers, so a name on this list is not proof of a working server.
  • Our copy is of one search of the register: the servers that say they charge per call. A server in the register that does not say so is not in front of us and is not counted anywhere on this page.
  • 17 of our 31 dated copies stopped at the first 100 names, because the register hands out 100 at a time and our reader did not ask for the second page. Every copy from 14 Aug 2026 onward is the whole list. On those days a name could have been on the register and missing from our copy.
  • 3 days inside our range have no copy at all: 8 Aug 2026, 18 Aug 2026, 19 Aug 2026. A name that appeared and went away entirely inside one of those gaps was never in front of us.
  • The date the register gives is the date the version it lists today was published. It moves forward every time a seller ships, so it is not the date that seller joined the register.
