How it works

  1. Email the street and the city. Do not send a person's name. We reply with how many permit rows we hold for that street.
  2. Pay once we have a checkout link, or email us first and we send that link in the same thread. There is no card button on this page yet.
  3. After you pay, a person emails you the packet the same day if that street is already in the six-city store. If we hold fewer than two rows, you do not pay.
