What this page cannot tell you

  • We can only show a change between two of our own reads. A vendor who changed a sentence and changed it back between one read and the next did something we never saw, and it is not on this page.
  • A page's fingerprint moving is not its wording moving. 420 pages came back different from the day before in this window; most of that was a session number, a menu, or the capitals in a heading, and produced no sentence anyone could quote. Those are not rows.
  • 211 passages on 19 pages moved one way and then moved back. That is one of the vendor's own servers disagreeing with another, not a decision, so both halves are left out.
  • We read the page the vendor publishes on the open web. The contract you actually signed can say something different, and we never see that.
  • The quote in the table is an excerpt, cut at about 210 characters around the part that moved. The file you buy carries the whole stored passage and the date on either side of it.
  • We only hold pages we can fetch. On 23 Aug 2026, 13 of the pages we ask for refused us outright and 8 tell any automated reader not to fetch them, so we do not. Every one of them is named on the coverage page.
  • We say what moved and when. We do not grade any company, we do not score one, and we do not tell you whether a change is good or bad for you. That is your lawyer's job, not ours.
  • This page carries notices that name a year. A vendor who writes “this model will be retired in due course” has told you nothing datable, and we would rather leave the row out than print a deadline nobody gave.
