What this feed is

A site’s robots file is the public note that tells crawlers what they may read. Publishers edit it in place, and nothing keeps the old version. Once the new file is up, yesterday’s rule is gone and no one can prove what it said.

You name the domains. We tell you who started or stopped blocking which AI crawler, and on which day.
