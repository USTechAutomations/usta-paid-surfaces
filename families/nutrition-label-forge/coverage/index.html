What this page cannot tell you

  • Added sugars is not in the USDA download we read: it holds 0 rows for it. The calculator asks you for that number rather than guessing it.
  • A USDA food is an average of samples, not your recipe. The panel it produces is a starting point that you check against your own formula.
  • 5 USDA categories are left out of the list entirely, named in the table above.
  • The eCFR is a continuously updated version of the CFR and is not the official legal edition.
  • Meat and poultry under the Federal Meat Inspection Act or the Poultry Products Inspection Act are labelled under USDA rules, not 21 CFR 101.9.
  • Nothing in this family tells you which paragraph applies to your product, or whether your label is compliant.
