Read this before anything else

This sample is the prototype running on local test data, not a live read of any vendor. The five names below (api-alpha, billing-beta, sdk-gamma, cli-delta, queue-epsilon) are fixtures we wrote. Nobody fetched OpenAI, Stripe, or anyone else for this page. A public board of about 25 real APIs is the product; picking those sources and confirming each vendor’s written terms still needs a person, and it has not been done.

We cannot backfill. A verdict needs two dated readings we actually took. If you start today, the earliest we can tell you anything is the second reading. A buyer who starts in March cannot ask in June what happened in April.

The first week after you pay is one reading and no verdict. That is not a trial we forgot to make useful. One reading cannot prove a change. We would rather say that now.

What you actually buy

The public board is free and stays free. The paid thing is a monthly watch of one named repo: up to 40 dependencies you choose, weekly readings, the full dated diffs, the verdicts, and any open forecast. A person emails you that file once a month for as long as the subscription runs.

A larger plan at $499 a month covers up to 200 dependencies with daily readings instead of weekly. That amount is not the rail price. Say so in the email if the repo is that size.

  • Not Dependabot or Renovate.Those tell you a newer version exists in a package registry. We keep a dated record of version, price, and the behaviour flags we can see, whether or not anyone published a release note.
  • Not a one-off audit.One reading cannot produce a verdict, so we will not sell a single-shot “audit” of your list.
  • Not a live vendor read, yet.JSON sources only, and only where a person has recorded that the written terms allow automated reads. A robots.txt allow does not override a terms page that forbids it. We do not scrape HTML marketing pages: a layout change would look like a price change, which is the lie this product exists to refuse.
  • Not unknown rounded up to unchanged.If we could not read it, or we only have one reading, the answer is unknown, with the reason.

Questions we are actually asked

What will I see in week one? Your first reading, and nothing else. From the second reading on, you get real comparisons.

Can you tell me what moved last quarter before I paid? No. We can only compare readings we took.

Why does it say unknown? We could not read the dependency, we only have one reading, or the evidence contradicted itself. All three are genuinely “we don’t know”.

How accurate are the forecasts? Whatever the board currently counts. On this fixture run it is 75.0 percent, 3 of 4, with 2 still open and excluded. A live board with no settled forecasts will say unknown.

Do you store our code? No. We store the list of dependencies you name and the readings we take of public sources.
