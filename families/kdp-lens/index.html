What this is

KDP Lens reads the Amazon.com page already in your tab. The 0.1.1 update separates an explicit overall Books or Kindle Store rank from category and list positions. If an overall rank is missing or ambiguous, it shows Unavailable. Search averages and publisher groupings are calculated locally. Page text stays on your machine; saved license keys may be rechecked by the entitlement service.

Every figure on this page is a demo overlay. These made-up values illustrate the prepared 0.1.1 update. They are not current Amazon observations or sales evidence. The update removes sales predictions and rank targets for reaching category #1.

What the prepared 0.1.1 update showsdemo

  • Book pageTitle, explicitly stated overall Books or Kindle Store rank, observed category placements, price, reviews, and publisher. Missing or conflicting overall rank is shown as Unavailable.
  • Best-seller listThe displayed list position and title for up to ten entries. A list position is not treated as an overall book rank.
  • Search resultsResult count and review averages from the parsed results, plus a heuristic grouping of up to ten entries by publisher. These summaries do not measure demand or predict sales.

What it never does

  • Never touches your KDP accountNo login, no dashboard scrape, no upload.
  • Never sends page textBook titles, ranks, and reviews stay on your machine. License verification sends your key to the entitlement service, including rechecks of a saved key.
  • Never a receiptRanks, review counts, and publisher groupings do not establish unit sales, revenue, or a route to category #1.
