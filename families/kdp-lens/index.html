What this is

KDP Lens is a browser add-on. It reads the Amazon.com page already in your tab. Rank-to-sales math runs in the browser. Page text does not leave the machine. The only network call is a license-key check, and only when you paste a key.

Every figure on this page is a demo overlay. It is not a live Amazon rank, not a KDP dashboard, and not a receipt. The sales curve is guessed and will be calibrated later from real data.

What you see on each page typedemo

  • Book pageTitle, Best Sellers Rank, estimated sales per day, category depth (the overall rank you would need to hit #1 in that category), price, reviews, publisher.
  • Best-seller listEach listed rank treated as that category's BSR, with estimated sales per day. Hitting #1 means rank 1 on that list.
  • Search resultsResult count, average reviews of the top 10, and the share of those ten that look indie vs a big publisher.
