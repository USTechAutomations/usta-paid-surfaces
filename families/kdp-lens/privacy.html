Privacy policy — KDP Lens

Plain-language notice for the KDP Lens browser add-on.

US Tech Automations, 3298 N Glassford Hill Rd Ste 104 PMB 1055, Prescott Valley AZ 86314.

KDP Lens is a browser extension. It reads the Amazon.com page already open in your tab so it can show estimated daily sales, category depth, and keyword competition. That page text stays on your machine. We do not send book titles, ranks, reviews, or any other page content to our servers.

The only network call the extension makes is a license-key check, and only when you paste a key into the popup. That request goes to our entitlement service with the key string. It does not include Amazon page text, your KDP login, your name, or your email.

We do not create a KDP account for you. We do not log into Amazon as you. We do not sell browsing data. A free daily counter (10 pages) is stored in your browser with chrome.storage.local. A successful key check is cached there for up to 7 days so the extension still works if you are offline.
