What this is

The EPA actor runs in your Apify account and returns structured drinking-water-system records. Apify handles execution, billing and export of the result dataset. The source data is public; the paid product is the extraction program and its structured output.

What you receive

System ID, name, town, population served, connections, water source, owner type and violation counts. Review the current input schema, output details and limits in the Apify listing. Our OSHA and NRC actors are deprecated and are not offered here as active products.

What these do not do

  • No private peopleThe subject is always a firm, a facility, a water system or an incident. A row that read as a private individual would be withheld, not renamed.
  • No home addressesTown and state only. No street line is ever kept or returned.
  • No claim to be the agencyNot affiliated with OSHA, the EPA or the US Coast Guard. Not legal, tax or professional advice.
  • Only as fresh as the sourceEach record is read live from the agency on the day of the run, and public records can lag the event they describe.
