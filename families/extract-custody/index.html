Read this before anything else

The free tool prints a profile and a retention receipt on your machine. The paid vault is a log of what was processed, signed with a key your client holds. We never see the files. After the run we store nothing.

Only the $9/mo solo vault is chargeable in the prototype today. There is no team price to take, and no pay button on this page yet. The buyer receives the custody log after you pay.

Your client checks this with the key you give them. A stranger cannot. Whoever holds that key can check the log — and could also sign with it. This is a record your client can check, not a public proof anyone can check.

“We store nothing” means our own folders, during the run. The guard watches the directories it is told to watch while extraction runs. It is not a claim about your machine, about anything before or after the call, or about memory. If you save the printed profile, that copy is yours.

A clean extraction is not an accurate one. The free tool matches patterns in English text. It will miss things, and it will pull out odd phrases. It is a starting point you edit. We prove we kept nothing; we do not prove the profile is complete or correct.

We are not a legal product. We produce a record. What you do with it is yours. No regulation is named here because we do not certify you against one.

The prototype then tampers with copies of the log on purpose. The client-key check is the one that matters, because we can re-sign our own half.

Catching a shortened log needs one fact on the client’s side: the last entry they signed. If they lose that, we still cannot edit a past entry, but a shorter log is harder to tell from an honest one. Keep that last entry. We cannot recover the key if it is lost — that is the same property that stops us forging it.

Questions we are actually asked

Do you ever see my client’s files? No. You send a fingerprint of the file and a short description of what you did. The file itself never leaves your machine.

Why should I believe the “we store nothing” part? Run the free tool with the network off and watch it work. It is the same guard the paid product runs behind. The receipt prints files written: 0, or it prints no profile.

Can a third party check this without the key? No. Handing someone the key lets them check — and also lets them sign. Do not call this a public proof.

What if I lose the key? You lose the ability to check the history, and the strongest part of the guarantee goes with it. Keep a backup. We cannot restore it.

Is this a certification? No. It is a record. Nobody has audited us and we do not claim otherwise.
