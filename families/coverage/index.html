What this page cannot tell you

  • A big number is not coverage of your thing.Millions of rows across a whole country can still hold nothing for the one county, company or product you care about. Name it and we will count it before you pay, not after.
  • This table is the readers, not the whole shop.AI policy and terms changes is listed in the directory and fed by no reader in this table, so nothing here counts them.
  • Every feed we sell is still being added to.Where that stops being true, this table says so on the row, with the date it stopped.
  • Row counts are counts, not quality.We count what we sealed. We do not claim the source was complete, correct or timely on the day we read it. That limit is real and we will not talk around it.
