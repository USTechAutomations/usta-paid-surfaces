What this page cannot tell you

  • These are the prices one marketplace publishes for each model, read from its own public model list. They are the sticker, not your bill. What you actually pay depends on how many tokens you send, which provider serves the request and any contract you already have, and we cannot see any of that.
  • This is what one marketplace publishes for Qwen. It is not a quote from Qwen and it is not a contract price. If you buy direct, your number may be a different number and we cannot see it.
  • We do not read the vendors' own price pages for these numbers. We do fetch some of those pages and we publish nothing out of them, because we have no permission to relay them. They are named on the coverage page and they are on hold.
  • A fingerprint of a web page is not a price. We also save a fingerprint of some vendors' own price pages, and the noisiest of them came back different across 64 of the 64 gaps between our 65 reads. A published model price moved across the newest of those gaps for ten models. A feed built out of page fingerprints would report a price change almost every day for almost every vendor, and almost every row would be wrong. Nothing on this page is built that way.
  • We report that a published number changed between two copies we sealed. We do not know why, we do not claim a vendor announced anything, and we never ring anybody up to ask. A marketplace sticker can also tick more than once in a week, and when it does we show every tick rather than the tidy version.
  • We hold 65 dated copies across the 74 days from 11 Jun 2026 to 23 Aug 2026, so there are 9 days in that window we hold nothing for. A price that moved and moved back inside one of those days is invisible to us, and so it is invisible to you. The days are listed on the coverage page.
