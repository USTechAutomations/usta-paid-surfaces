Doing this yourself

The list we read is OpenRouter’s own machine-readable model list — the file its marketplace publishes for software to read. It carries today’s number and only today’s. When a vendor changes what it charges, the old figure is overwritten and there is no changelog, no announcement and nothing left to point at.

To catch that yourself you would have to read that list every day and keep every day’s answer dated and readable. One missed day is one price move you cannot prove. That is the whole job, and it is the only job this feed does — we are not fingerprinting anybody’s marketing page, and no number we sell you comes from one.

What this page cannot tell you

These are one marketplace’s published prices, not your bill. They are the sticker on a public model list. What you actually pay depends on how many tokens you send and which provider serves the request. It is not a quote from the model’s maker and it is not a contract price.

We do not read the vendors’ own price pages for these numbers. We fetch some of those pages and publish nothing out of them, because we have no permission to relay them. A fingerprint of a web page is not a price, and we never treat one as if it were.

We report that a number moved. We do not know why. We never ring anybody up to ask, and we do not claim a vendor announced anything. There are also days inside our range with no copy at all; each page below lists its own missing days by date, and a price that moved and moved back inside one of them is invisible to us.
