Doing this yourself

The list this feed read is a marketplace’s own machine-readable model list — the file it publishes for software to read. It carries today’s number and only today’s. When a vendor changes what it charges, the old figure is overwritten and there is no changelog, no announcement and nothing left to point at.

To catch that yourself you would have to read that list every day and keep every day’s answer dated and readable. One missed day is one price move you cannot prove. That is the whole job, and it is the only job this feed did — we are not fingerprinting anybody’s marketing page, and no number on this page comes from one.

What this page cannot tell you

These are one marketplace’s published prices, not your bill. They are the sticker on a public model list. What you actually pay depends on how many tokens you send and which provider serves the request. It is not a quote from the model’s maker and it is not a contract price.

We do not read the vendors’ own price pages for these numbers. We fetch some of those pages and publish nothing out of them, because we have no permission to relay them. A fingerprint of a web page is not a price, and we never treat one as if it were.

We report that a number moved. We do not know why. We never ring anybody up to ask, and we do not claim a vendor announced anything. There are also days inside our range with no copy at all; each page below lists its own missing days by date, and a price that moved and moved back inside one of them is invisible to us.

There is no sample file here any more

We took it down on 24 Aug 2026. Until that day this page handed out 25 rows of the feed as a free file. It was a small compilation cut from one publisher’s list, and that publisher’s written terms forbid a machine copying it. A feed nobody can buy has no reason to keep giving that away, so the file was removed and the two links to it went with it. Anyone who already downloaded it still has it; we cannot unsend a file.

The card for this feed in the directory says “sample not ready”. That is the nearest label this site has and it is not quite the right one: nothing failed and nothing is being prepared. The file was taken down on purpose and it is not coming back.

Nothing on this page is for sale, and nothing on it costs anything to read.
