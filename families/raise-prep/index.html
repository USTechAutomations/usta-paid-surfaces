Who this pack is for

Someone who already has a pay conversation in the diary, inside the next 14 days, and wants the answers ready before they walk in. Not a job seeker shopping in general. Not someone who wants us to email their boss.

If the meeting is not booked, this pack is the wrong product. After the talk, it is worth nothing to you.

What you get

One written pack, for one named talk, in four parts. After you pay — which does not happen on this page — a person emails you that pack. It is coach-only notes. It is not a script that acts in your name.

  • What the market says.Range, middle, source, sample size, and date, when we have them. If we do not have figures for your role and city, there is no pack and no charge. Numbers from the prototype’s fixture table are not published here.
  • Your talking script.Cues for you to open, state the ask once, put evidence on the table, and close on a next step. You say the lines. We do not.
  • Counter-moves.The five things a manager tends to say back, why each one works on you, what to say, and what to settle for if that fails.
  • The coach-only terms.In writing, in the pack: you are the only person in this negotiation. Nothing is sent to the other side.

The five answers a manager gives, and why each one lands. From the engine. No wage figures. prototype demo, 25 Aug 2026
#They sayWhy it lands
1“There’s no budget this cycle.”It sounds final and it moves the problem to someone who isn’t in the room.
2“You’re already paid fairly for your level.”It reframes your ask as a misunderstanding rather than a request.
3“Let’s revisit at your annual review.”It buys months and resets the conversation with no cost to them.
4“A title change is easier than a raise right now.”It offers you something that feels like progress and costs them nothing.
5“Other people at your level earn the same.”It makes your ask sound like a demand for special treatment.

The paid pack also has the rebuttal and the fallback for each of those five. They are not on this free page.
