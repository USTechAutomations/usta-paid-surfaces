What this page is23 rows on this page · newest sealed read 2026-08-25

Newest sealed read: 2026-08-25. We read this source every day. We hold 1,105 sealed runs going back to 2026-06-07.

  • 19 of our readers are switched off right now, on purpose. 17 are still running.Each switched-off one has a dated note saying who decided it and why. It keeps everything it already sealed and adds nothing. The table below is all of them. 8 of the 19 fed a page in the directory and so appear in the table on the coverage page too, where they are counted as 8, not 19. The other 11 never fed one, which is why that page does not list them and this one does.
  • 21 sources refuse in their own published rules, so we do not read them.Recorded on 21 Aug 2026. The reasons are things like terms that forbid automated reading, a licence that allows non-commercial use only, and one law firm’s page naming individuals whose homes were repossessed. We have a lawful stand-in for one of them written down, and we have not built it.
  • One state source told us no this month. We treated that as no.Arizona’s environment-permit layer. Three checks on 21 Aug 2026, all of them failed, so we stopped. It is very likely a public record. Likely is not evidence, and we do not collect on likely.
  • A missing rules file is not permission, and neither is a page that loads.The water service publishes no rules file at all — it answers 404, which is a clear "there are no restrictions here", and we may read it. The Arizona host answered 403, which is not an answer at all, so we treated it as a no. A page that loads is not the same as being allowed to keep what is on it.
  • We slow down when a source tells us to.Reading the water service once a second, it started refusing us after about eighteen requests and we finished only 49 of 104 sources. We slowed to one request every five seconds and finished 104 of 104, 69,133 rows, in about 9 minutes. The fix for a source pushing back is to ask more slowly, never to get around it.
  • Nobody here writes their own permission slip.A source is only read after a person has checked it and written a dated note. Software cannot write that note for itself, and a source with no note stays shut. That is the whole reason the list above exists.

What this page cannot tell you

  • These are our own dated decisions, not legal advice.They record what we checked, on what day, and what we decided. Your own lawyer should read them as our working, not as their conclusion.
  • The 21 sources that refuse are not named here.Each one has a written reason in our notes. If you want to know whether a source you care about is one of them, ask and we will tell you.
  • A decision can be revisited, and a source can change its mind.Every note above carries the condition that would make us look again. Nothing on this page is a promise never to collect something; it is a record of what we will not do today, and why.
