What it does

You paste your rows from a supplier statement, in whatever rough form you have them. You get a link back for yourself, and a second link to send to the other business. Once they paste their rows too, the tool lines the two sides up and shows exactly which invoices do not match. The report shows differences. It never posts entries or moves money. It only compares two lists you both typed in.

What this is not

The report shows differences. It never posts entries or moves money. Nothing you do here touches any accounting system or bank.
