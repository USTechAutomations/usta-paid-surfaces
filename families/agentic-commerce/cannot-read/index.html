What this page cannot tell you

  • A refusal is what our reader saw, not a fault. Blocking an unfamiliar reader is an ordinary thing for a large shop to do, and we name our reader and give a contact address in every request.
  • We cannot tell you whether these companies serve any of the seven files. They may serve all of them. We can only tell you we were not allowed to look.
  • We do not retry within a day and we do not change our reader to get around a block. That keeps the record honest and it is why this list stays roughly the same size from day to day.
  • The panel is a fixed list of 276 company websites, frozen before collection started. A company that is not on that list is not missing from this feed — we have never asked it anything. Email operations@ustechautomations.com with the companies you follow and we will tell you which of them we already hold.
