Privacy policy — Grant Fit

Plain-language notice for the Grant Fit browser add-on.

Effective date: 7 September 2026.

Grant Fit is a browser extension from US Tech Automations (Prescott Valley, AZ).

What it reads

It reads the active tab only when you click Check this page. It does not scan other tabs, and it does not run in the background on sites you visit.

What it sends

The extracted page text (address, title, and main text) and the org profile you saved are sent to our scoring server so we can return a fit report. If you paste a license key, that key is sent to our entitlement server to check that it is still valid.

What it stores on your computer

Your org profile, license key, a random install id, the monthly free-report counter, and a short cache of a good key check. That cache is kept up to 7 days so a report can still run if you are briefly offline.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not put a person's name or email in our product logs. We do not use the page text to contact the funder. We do not run ads.
