Grant Fit: eligibility and fit score for any funding page

A Chrome extension. On a funding page, one click returns eligible yes, no, or unclear, a fit score from 0 to 100 against the org profile you saved, the deadline, the match, and the reasons. Five reports a month are free. Paid is $19/mo. The report below is a demo, labeled as a demo. This is not a promise a funder will pay, and it is not legal advice.

What this is

You keep a funding page open. You click once. Grant Fit reads that tab, scores it against the org you saved, and shows the fit report in the popup. It does not write the grant, it does not file it, and it does not contact the funder.

The numbers in the report below are a demo. They are not a real applicant and not a real award.
