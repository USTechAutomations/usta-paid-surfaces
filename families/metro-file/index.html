What this cannot tell you

It is not the city's full board. We sell the status-change record across the sealed days we hold, not every permit the city has ever published.

It is not a feed. There is no next month's file unless you buy again.

A hole is a day we did not seal, not a day the city published nothing. The coverage CSV names those days. The six shared holes on Austin, Cincinnati, New York and San Francisco are 2026-06-19, 2026-06-20, 2026-06-21, 2026-07-04, 2026-07-15 and 2026-08-08.
