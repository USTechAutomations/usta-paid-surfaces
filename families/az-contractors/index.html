Why this one is not available

We cannot collect the Arizona roster, so there is nothing to sell. The state’s public licence list sits behind a bot filter that refuses our requests. We could get around it. We are not going to: working around a block that a public body deliberately put up is not something we will do to make a page look finished.

So this page has no price and no sample. It is here because we said we would build it, and taking the page down quietly would hide the reason it never shipped.

What would have to change

One of two things. Either Arizona publishes the roster in a way that permits automated collection, or we are given written permission to collect it. Until one of those happens, we hold no dated copies of this list at all — not a partial set, not an old set, none.

We look at this again in November 2026. If it is still blocked then, this page will still say so.

What to buy instead
