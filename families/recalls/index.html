What we actually hold

292 separate recalls, kept as 5,738 dated copies taken on 58 separate days between 23 Jun 2026 and 21 Aug 2026. By class that is Class I 133, Class II 127, Class III 32. The companies sit in 29 states and territories, and the run log records 61 finished collection runs.

This is food, and only food. These are the food enforcement reports the US Food and Drug Administration publishes. No drug recalls, no medical device recalls, and nothing from any other agency. If you need those, say so and we will tell you honestly that we do not have them yet.

Each read takes the newest recalls the agency is listing, about a hundred of them, not the whole history. A recall that dropped off that list before our next read is not in here.

45 rows came back with no recall number. They are left out of every count on this page. Rows with no number cannot be told apart from each other, so a change they appear to show is really two different recalls being mistaken for one. We found exactly that while building this page, and it is why the class-change number above is zero rather than one.

2 days did not work. Between 23 Jun 2026 and 21 Aug 2026 there are 2 days with nothing sealed at all. We print the gap rather than let you find it later.

Doing this yourself

You can search the agency’s enforcement list today and it will give you every recall as it stands right now. What it will not give you is the same record as it stood three weeks ago. The status field is rewritten in place, and the word that was there before is gone.

To have what is on this page you would have to pull the list every day, store every copy, and compare your own copies against each other. Miss a fortnight and the recalls that were posted and closed inside it never show up in any later file.

What is in the copies we hold

  • Every recall whose status or class movedRecall number, company, product, the word before, the word after, and both of the dates we read it.
  • Every recall that turned up since the read beforeWith the date the company started it, so a slow listing does not read as a fresh recall.
  • One class or one state you nameNot the whole national list you would have to cut down yourself.
  • The days we missed, namedEvery file says which days we could not read, so a gap never reads as a quiet week.

What this cannot tell you. Anything that happened after 21 Aug 2026. A recall posted since then is not in here, and a status that moved since then moved without us.

How it works

  1. You email us and say which class or which state you follow.
  2. We reply with what our dated copies hold for it, which days are missing, and the date of our last copy. There is nothing monthly to buy while the reading is stopped.
  3. A person emails you the what-moved file, and names anything we could not collect.
