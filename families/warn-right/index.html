What this is

WarnRight is a Shopify app. It reads a product title, materials, and category. A word list flags common listed chemicals. If the list is unsure, it calls the Fit API task prop65_classify. A person in the shop still confirms or overrides the draft. A theme app extension prints warnright.warning_text on the product page.

This page does not certify a store and does not give legal advice. A confirmed warning is a stored string. It is not a legal finding.
