# Attention ecosystem and AI Transition Hub: investment audit

Assessment date: 12 September 2026. Direction audit; no implementation, publication, scheduling, sending, spending, or account changes. Recommendations below are proposed resource allocations, not measured returns. Source checkout: `usta-paid-surfaces` at `f89520ae`; `lead-outreach` at `cfd7b81`. The parent `Claude CLI` directory is not a Git repository.

**Invest in a narrow AI employee pilot and lightweight proof for inbound prospects. Reuse the existing breakthrough analyzer for that pilot. Test workshops before building a community. Defer the broad Transition Hub and multimedia factory. Reject concealed promotional accounts.**

Growing through many routes is compatible with sharing one delivery foundation. Each new route should add a buyer, a recurring problem, or a distribution channel; generating more formats for the same unsolicited pitch adds production work without establishing another revenue path.

## Correct the operating assumptions

**The throughput arithmetic does not support the proposal.** Eighteen hours is 1,080 minutes. Serial ideal capacities are 216 five-minute packets, 72 fifteen-minute packets, 18 one-hour packets, or six three-hour packets. An illustrative 80% cold / 15% warm / 5% hot mix costs `0.8×15 + 0.15×60 + 0.05×180 = 30` minutes per lead: 36 leads/day before retries and review. A serial 300/day target requires 3.6 minutes each. Parallelism can improve throughput; it does not eliminate work or establish the benchmark.

Five minutes of **human** time is a different target: even one minute reviewing each of 216 packets consumes 3.6 hours daily. Batching does not remove fact checking. Start with ten drafts, not an eighteen-hour production schedule.

The claimed 80–90% reliability, 70% open rate, 35% retention, 99% automation, and six-/twelve-month performance forecasts have no supporting measurements here. Do not use them as business-case inputs. As an illustrative calculation only, twenty independent steps that each succeed 99% of the time yield about 81.8% all-step success. Independence itself is not guaranteed.

Do not promote opens to warm status. Apple Mail Privacy Protection limits the sender's ability to observe opening; a raw link fetch may also be a security scanner. Prefer an explicit reply, completed intake, requested demonstration, or payment. [Apple privacy explanation](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/mail-privacy-protection/)

Spark capacity is available at the metadata layer, but inference latency, queue contention, sustained thermal behavior, and power consumption were not benchmarked. NVIDIA lists 128GB unified memory; hardware specifications do not guarantee this application's throughput. The old `:30000` endpoint refused connection; later inspection identified it as the retired pooled endpoint, not evidence that the four active endpoints were unavailable. Do not reinstall or replace models based on the old plan. Local doctrine also reserves customer-facing copy for the authorized commercial route; use local models for extraction and internal classification. [NVIDIA hardware documentation](https://docs.nvidia.com/dgx/dgx-spark/hardware.html)

Hume usage has its own billing and allowance constraints. Do not assume local hardware makes Hume free, or enable usage without confirming a permitted free allowance. [Hume billing](https://dev.hume.ai/docs/resources/billing)

Unattended scheduling must respect the existing 10:00–15:00 MST quiet window. A workload should run because eligible work and review capacity exist, with backpressure and resource limits. Eighteen hours is not an intrinsic hardware-safety threshold.

## Concrete proposed investment

Authorize a future **five-engineer-day discovery/pilot budget**, no incremental paid services. This audit does not start that build:

- **Three days:** establish a narrow AI employee around one existing recurring task. First candidate: supplier statement exception preparation using LedgerMatch. Proposed workflow: permitted input → schema checks → invoice matching → exception report → human-review draft → outcome receipt. No automatic payments, vendor messages, or accounting-system writebacks. Confirm buyer need and current delivery behavior before selecting it conclusively.
- **One day:** ten inbound/warm proof packets. Each contains two source-backed facts, one labeled operational hypothesis, one relevant reusable example, and one clear next step. Measure end-to-end generation and operator correction time. Build no per-prospect app.
- **Half a day:** reuse the breakthrough analyzer to identify improvements to that exact task. Retain source dates, license information, operational requirements, limitations, and an explicit recommendation to ignore irrelevant releases.
- **Half a day:** prepare one repeatable workshop demonstration and operator invitation drafts. Topic: solve the same narrow problem on permitted or synthetic data. Session delivery is additional human time; invitations remain human-sent.

This creates several testable routes around shared capabilities: a recurring workflow product, a fixed workshop service, reusable proof for inbound conversion, and eventually a useful member network. A successful route can finance adjacent paths without commissioning the whole hub.

Measure contribution as collected revenue minus incremental delivery costs and valued human time. Compute cost alone is not acquisition cost. Use revenue per qualified opportunity and per operator hour, repeat usage, correction rate, and fulfilled orders. Do not infer revenue odds from a quality score.

Proposed continuation signals: three independent target buyers confirm the same repeated problem, at least one requests the concrete paid scope, and a permitted-input replay demonstrates the promised result. These are discovery signals, not proof of market size. Scale delivery after an attributed payment and successful fulfillment, then seek replication across buyers. The standing 9/10 quality and 70% first-revenue cohort targets remain targets, not guarantees.

Track fixed 14-day qualified-opportunity cohorts with numerator, denominator, source, product, and payment attribution; persist post-publication and 72-hour observations. Missing telemetry is UNKNOWN. No sales in a tiny or unexposed cohort calls for diagnosis, not automatic termination. Stop extra build work at the time cap if there is no buyer signal; revise distribution or scope before adding formats.
